# Overview

This document defines the monthly reconciliation process for GuideMyTank.

The purpose of reconciliation is to ensure that business banking records, bookkeeping records, accounting records, and supporting documentation remain accurate and complete before closing each month.

GuideMyTank uses a hybrid bookkeeping workflow consisting of:

- Novo Business Checking
- Google Sheets Financial Dashboard
- Wave Accounting Starter

Google Sheets serves as the operational bookkeeping dashboard while Wave Accounting serves as the official accounting system of record.

Monthly reconciliation ensures that all three systems remain synchronized.

---

# Objectives

The monthly reconciliation process is designed to:

- Verify the accuracy of all financial records.
- Ensure all business income and expenses have been recorded.
- Verify Google Sheets matches Wave Accounting.
- Confirm Novo account activity matches accounting records.
- Detect missing or duplicate transactions.
- Maintain audit-ready financial records.
- Prepare the business for tax reporting and financial analysis.

---

# Frequency

Reconciliation should be completed:

- Once each month
- After all expected deposits and expenses have cleared
- Before monthly financial reports are finalized
- Before any owner draw is taken

# Monthly Reconciliation Checklist

## 1. Banking Review

Review Novo Business Checking.

Verify:

- Beginning balance
- Ending balance
- Deposits
- Withdrawals
- Transfers
- No unauthorized transactions

Confirm all business activity has been recorded.

---

## 2. Google Sheets Review

Review the Transactions ledger.

Verify:

- Every transaction has been entered.
- Dates are correct.
- Vendors are correct.
- Categories are assigned.
- Payment accounts are assigned.
- Business-use percentages are accurate.
- Business amounts calculate correctly.
- Receipt status is current.
- Wave upload status is correct.

---

## 3. Wave Accounting Review

Within Wave Accounting:

- Import any remaining transactions.
- Categorize imported transactions.
- Verify payment accounts.
- Review Owner Investments.
- Review Owner Draws.
- Resolve duplicate transactions.
- Resolve uncategorized transactions.

Wave should match the Google Sheets ledger before closing the month.

---

## 4. Revenue Review

Verify all expected income has been received, including:

- Google AdSense
- Amazon Associates
- Affiliate partnerships
- Sponsorships
- Digital products
- Subscription revenue
- Interest income
- Other business income

Investigate any missing or unexpected deposits.

---

## 5. Expense Review

Review every expense to ensure it:

- Is a legitimate business expense.
- Uses the correct expense category.
- Has an assigned payment account.
- Has an appropriate business-use percentage.
- Includes supporting documentation.
- Has not been duplicated.

---

## 6. Documentation Review

Verify supporting documentation exists where applicable.

Examples include:

- Bank statements
- Receipts
- Invoices
- Payment confirmations
- Advertising reports
- Affiliate reports

---

## 7. Financial Reports

Generate and review:

- Profit & Loss Statement
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement

Verify reports accurately reflect business activity for the month.

---

## 8. Tax Reserve Review

Estimate taxes based on current business performance.

Review the Tax Reserve worksheet and confirm sufficient funds have been reserved for future tax obligations.

---

## 9. Owner Draw Review

Before taking an owner draw, verify:

- Monthly reconciliation is complete.
- Financial reports have been reviewed.
- Business expenses have been paid.
- Tax reserves are adequate.
- Sufficient operating cash remains.

If these conditions are not exist, postpone the owner draw.

---

## 10. Monthly Close

The accounting period may be considered complete when:

- Novo account activity has been reviewed.
- Google Sheets matches Wave Accounting.
- All income has been verified.
- All expenses have been categorized.
- Receipts have been retained.
- Financial reports have been reviewed.
- Outstanding discrepancies have been resolved.

---

# Exception Handling

If discrepancies are discovered:

1. Identify the affected transaction.
2. Determine whether the issue exists in Novo, Google Sheets, or Wave.
3. Correct the affected records.
4. Verify supporting documentation.
5. Document significant corrections when appropriate.

The monthly reconciliation should not be considered complete until all material discrepancies have been resolved.
