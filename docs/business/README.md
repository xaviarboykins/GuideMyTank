# GuideMyTank Business Documentation

This directory contains the business, financial, and operational documentation for GuideMyTank.

The purpose of this documentation is to establish standardized business procedures, maintain accurate financial records, and provide a single source of truth for the operational side of the business.

As GuideMyTank grows, these documents should be reviewed and updated to reflect changes in business operations, monetization, accounting practices, and legal structure.

---

# Business Overview

GuideMyTank is currently operated as a **California Sole Proprietorship**.

Business decisions documented within this directory support the long-term goals of building a sustainable online business while maintaining accurate financial records and preparing for future growth.

Current business foundation:

- Business Structure: Sole Proprietorship
- Business Name: GuideMyTank
- Federal Tax ID: Employer Identification Number (EIN)
- California Registration: Fictitious Business Name (FBN/DBA) — In Progress
- Business Banking: Bluevine Business Checking
- Accounting Platform: Wave Accounting Starter
- Bookkeeping Dashboard: Google Sheets Financial Dashboard

# Guiding Principles

GuideMyTank follows the following business principles:

- Maintain complete separation between business and personal finances.
- Record all business income and expenses accurately.
- Keep financial records current and organized.
- Maintain documentation for significant business decisions.
- Use repeatable and well-documented financial workflows.
- Preserve complete audit trails for financial activity.
- Prioritize long-term business growth over short-term owner compensation.
- Build business infrastructure that can scale as the company grows.
