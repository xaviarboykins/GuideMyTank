# Overview

This document defines the standard process for tracking all revenue generated by GuideMyTank.

The objective is to ensure every dollar earned by the business is accurately recorded, verified, categorized, reconciled, and reported.

GuideMyTank uses a hybrid bookkeeping workflow consisting of:

- Novo Business Checking
- Google Sheets Financial Dashboard
- Wave Accounting Starter

Google Sheets serves as the operational revenue tracking system while Wave Accounting serves as the official accounting system of record.

---

# Objectives

The revenue tracking process is designed to:

- Capture every source of business income.
- Ensure all revenue is deposited into the business checking account.
- Maintain complete financial records.
- Categorize revenue consistently.
- Support accurate financial reporting.
- Prepare the business for tax compliance.
- Maintain an audit trail for all revenue received.

---

# Revenue Sources

GuideMyTank may generate revenue from multiple sources.

- Other affiliate partnerships

- Premium memberships
- Subscription services

# Revenue Workflow

All business revenue follows the same standardized workflow.

```text
Revenue Generated
        │
        ▼
Platform Records Earnings
        │
        ▼
Platform Issues Payment
        │
        ▼
Novo Business Checking
        │
        ▼
Record Deposit in Google Sheets
        │
        ▼
Assign Revenue Category
        │
        ▼
Wave Connect Import
        │
        ▼
Wave Accounting
        │
        ▼
Monthly Reconciliation
        │
        ▼
Financial Reporting
```

Google Sheets tracks operational bookkeeping.

Wave Accounting maintains the official financial records.

Each deposit should be categorized according to its source.

| Revenue Source | Accounting Category |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Google AdSense | Advertising Revenue |
| Amazon Associates | Affiliate Revenue |
| Other Affiliate Programs | Affiliate Revenue |
| Interest Earned | Interest Income |
| Sponsored Content | Advertising Revenue* |
| Digital Products | Other Business Income* |
| Memberships | Other Business Income* |
| Donations | Other Business Income |

\*Additional revenue accounts may be created in the future if these revenue streams become significant.

---

# Revenue Recording

Each revenue transaction recorded in Google Sheets should include:

- Date received
- Reference number (if applicable)
- Revenue source
- Description
- Revenue category
- Gross amount
- Payment account
- Wave import status
- Notes

All revenue should be recorded before month-end reconciliation.

---

# Revenue Verification

Each payment received should be verified against the originating platform.

Verification includes:

- Expected payment amount
- Payment date
- Deposit received in Novo
- Platform payment report
- Google Sheets transaction
- Wave Accounting transaction
- Correct revenue category

Discrepancies should be investigated immediately.

---

# Supporting Documentation

Maintain supporting documentation for all revenue received.

Examples include:

- Google AdSense payment reports
- Amazon Associates payment reports
- Affiliate network reports
- Sales reports
- Sponsorship agreements
- Subscription reports
- Bank deposit confirmations

Supporting documentation should be retained with financial records.

---

# Monthly Revenue Review

At least once each month:

- Review all revenue sources.
- Confirm expected payments were received.
- Verify all deposits have been recorded in Google Sheets.
- Import outstanding revenue into Wave.
- Verify revenue categories.
- Investigate missing or unexpected deposits.
- Update financial records if necessary.

---

# Missing or Incorrect Payments

If an expected payment is not received:

1. Verify the platform payment report.
2. Confirm payment status.
3. Check Novo Business Checking.
4. Verify Google Sheets records.
5. Verify Wave Accounting.
6. Contact the payment platform if necessary.
7. Document the resolution.

---

# Financial Reporting

Revenue contributes directly to:

- Profit & Loss Statement
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement
- Annual Financial Statements
- Tax Reporting
- Business Performance Analysis

Revenue reporting should always agree between:

- Novo Business Checking
- Google Sheets Financial Dashboard
- Wave Accounting
