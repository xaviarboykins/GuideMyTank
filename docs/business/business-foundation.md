# Overview

This document records the business formation and banking decisions made during the initial setup of GuideMyTank.

GuideMyTank will launch as a **sole proprietorship** to minimize startup costs while establishing a professional financial foundation. The business maintains a dedicated business checking account and a federal Employer Identification Number (EIN). California Fictitious Business Name (FBN/DBA) registration is currently in progress as part of the business formation process. Accounting and monetization systems are implemented separately through the Accounting Foundation.

These decisions are intended to support future monetization through:

- Google AdSense
- Amazon Associates
- Future affiliate partnerships
- Digital products
- Potential subscription services

This document serves as the official record of the business foundation decisions for the project.

---

# Business Structure

## Selected Structure

**Sole Proprietorship**

### Reasoning

GuideMyTank is currently operated by a single owner and does not require the additional legal or administrative complexity of forming an LLC during the early development stage.

Launching as a sole proprietorship provides several advantages:

- Lowest startup cost
- Minimal administrative overhead
- Simple tax reporting
- Fast business setup
- Easy transition to an LLC in the future if business needs change

The business structure will be re-evaluated after GuideMyTank reaches consistent revenue or if legal, financial, or liability considerations justify conversion to an LLC.

---

# Federal Tax Identification

## Decision

**Obtain a Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN)**

### Reasoning

Although a sole proprietor may legally operate using a Social Security Number (SSN), GuideMyTank will obtain a dedicated EIN from the Internal Revenue Service.

Using an EIN provides several long-term benefits:

- Reduces the need to share the owner's Social Security Number
- Creates a more professional business identity
- Simplifies banking relationships
- Supports future advertising and affiliate programs
- Provides a cleaner transition if the business converts to an LLC

The EIN will become the primary federal tax identification number used for business banking and future financial services.

---

# Business Name Registration

## Decision

Register "GuideMyTank" as a California Fictitious Business Name (FBN/DBA).

**Status:** In Progress

### Reasoning

GuideMyTank operates under a business name that does not include the owner's legal surname.

California law generally requires sole proprietors operating under a trade name to register a **Fictitious Business Name (FBN)**, commonly referred to as a **DBA (Doing Business As).**

Once finalized, registering the business name allows GuideMyTank to:

- Operate publicly under the GuideMyTank brand
- Open business banking using the business name
- Receive payments under the business name
- Maintain a consistent professional identity

# Business Banking

## Selected Provider

**Bluevine Business Checking**

### Alternatives Evaluated

- Bluevine
- Novo
- Mercury
- Lili

### Decision

Bluevine Business Checking was selected as the official banking platform for GuideMyTank.

### Reasoning

Bluevine best aligns with the operational and financial needs of GuideMyTank during its sole proprietorship stage.

Key factors influencing the decision include:

- No monthly maintenance fees
- Supports sole proprietors
- No minimum balance requirement
- Dedicated business checking
- Modern online and mobile banking
- FDIC insurance through partner banks
- Integrates well with accounting workflows
- Appropriate for online businesses
- Supports future advertising and affiliate revenue

Novo was initially selected during planning but the account application was not approved. Bluevine was subsequently approved and adopted as the production banking platform.

Mercury remains a strong alternative for future business growth but is generally better suited for incorporated startups.

Lili was not selected because several advanced features overlap with the existing accounting workflow provided by Wave Accounting and Google Sheets.
