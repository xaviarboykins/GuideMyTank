---

# Purpose

This document defines the policy governing owner compensation for GuideMyTank while operating as a sole proprietorship.

The objective is to ensure that business growth, financial stability, and tax obligations are prioritized before the owner receives compensation.

---

# Guiding Principles

GuideMyTank will prioritize long-term business growth over owner compensation during the early stages of the business.

Owner draws should only be made after:

- All business operating expenses have been paid.
- Estimated tax reserves have been set aside.
- Monthly bookkeeping has been completed.
- Monthly bank reconciliation has been completed.
- Financial reports have been reviewed.
- Sufficient operating cash remains to support ongoing business operations.

---

# Payment Schedule

Owner draws should occur **no more than once per month** following completion of the monthly financial close.

Recommended monthly workflow:

1. Review monthly financial statements.
2. Complete monthly reconciliation.
3. Verify all business expenses have been recorded.
4. Review available operating cash.
5. Confirm tax reserves are sufficient.
6. Transfer owner draw from the Bluevine Business Checking account.
7. Record the owner draw in Wave Accounting.

---

# Payment Priority

Business revenue should be allocated in the following order:

1. Business operating expenses
2. Tax reserves
3. Business growth and reinvestment
4. Owner draw

Business growth should take priority over owner compensation whenever practical during the early stages of the business.

---

# Minimum Cash Reserve

Maintain a minimum operating reserve before taking an owner draw.

Initial goal:

- One month of operating expenses

Long-term goal:

- Three to six months of operating expenses

---

# Owner Draw Guidelines

Owner draws:

- Are distributions of business profits to the owner.
- Are not business expenses.
- Must be recorded using the **Owner Draw** equity account in Wave Accounting.
- Should always be supported by completed bookkeeping and reconciliation.

---

# Review

This policy should be reviewed whenever:

- The business structure changes.
- GuideMyTank converts to an LLC or corporation.
- Business revenue grows significantly.
- The owner adopts a formal payroll system.
