# Species Factual Review — Batch 4: Bottom Dwellers

Date: July 2026

This local-only Phase 11I batch reviewed Cory relatives, loaches, plecos, algae grazers, bottom-oriented specialists, and associated invertebrates.

## Corrections

- Peppered Cory: `Corydoras paleatus` → accepted `Hoplisoma paleatum`; old name retained as an alias.
- Panda Cory: `Corydoras panda` → accepted `Hoplisoma panda`; old name retained as an alias.
- Mystery Snail: `Pomacea bridgesii` → `Pomacea diffusa`; the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service notes that aquarium-market `P. diffusa` is commonly misidentified as `P. bridgesii`.
- Crystal Red Shrimp: `Caridina cantonensis` → `Caridina logemanni`; the older trade identification remains an alias.
- Weather Loach: flow preference changed from moderate to low because it favors still or slow water with soft or muddy substrate.

## Confirmed risk traits

- Cory relatives, Kuhli Loaches, Otocinclus, and botiid loaches retain meaningful group requirements.
- Reticulated Hillstream Loach retains high-flow requirements.
- Clown Loach retains its six-fish local group requirement; FishBase describes groups of at least five and a much larger adult size than ordinary community loaches.
- Rainbow and Red Tail Sharks retain solitary, large bottom-territory traits.
- Rope Fish, Senegal Bichir, and Black Ghost Knifefish remain predation-risk specialists.

## Deferred

- Common trade “Bristlenose Pleco” may represent more than `Ancistrus cirrhosus`.
- Retail Otocinclus are often mixed or misidentified species.
- Exact social minimums for ornamental shrimp describe colony welfare rather than true schooling and may eventually need a distinct field.

No Supabase synchronization was performed.
