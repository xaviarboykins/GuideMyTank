# Species Factual Review — Batch 5: Schooling Community Fish

Date: July 2026

This local-only Phase 11I batch reviewed tetras, rasboras, danios, barbs, rainbowfish, and other schooling community fish.

## Corrections

- Buenos Aires Tetra: `Hyphessobrycon anisitsi` → accepted `Psalidodon anisitsi`; family updated to `Acestrorhamphidae`; historical name retained as an alias.
- Black Neon, Lemon, Serpae, and X-Ray Tetras: family updated from the older broad `Characidae` placement to `Acestrorhamphidae`, following the taxonomy used by current FishBase.

## Compatibility-critical findings

- Serpae Tetra, Tiger Barb, and Buenos Aires Tetra appropriately retain fin-nipping or semi-aggressive traits.
- Peaceful schooling species retain minimum group requirements rather than receiving artificial compatibility bonuses merely because their water ranges overlap.
- Existing cool-water versus tropical distinctions remain important for White Clouds, Zebra Danios, Rosy Barbs, and tropical tetras.
- The current records already distinguish active or boisterous schooling fish from calm, delicate species.

## Deferred

- Cherry Barb has recently been placed in `Rohanella` by some authorities while FishBase still uses `Puntius`; the stable trade-facing name is retained pending broader database adoption.
- Some modern characin family assignments remain in active revision. Scientific aliases should be preserved when names change.
- Group minimums are husbandry recommendations, not evidence that every two schooling species are compatible.

No Supabase synchronization was performed.
