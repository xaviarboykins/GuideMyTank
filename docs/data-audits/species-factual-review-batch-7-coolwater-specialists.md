# Species Factual Review — Batch 7: Cool-Water and Flow Specialists

Date: July 2026

This local-only Phase 11I batch reviewed goldfish varieties, cool-water community fish, and stream specialists.

## Corrections

- Common Goldfish: `competitive_feeder` changed to `true`. OATA specifically warns that faster common goldfish often outcompete fancy varieties for food.
- American Flagfish: flow preference changed from moderate to low. FishBase and the Florida Museum describe vegetated sloughs, ponds, lakes, canals, and sluggish streams.

## Confirmed distinctions

- Common Goldfish remain active, robust, and large-tank fish.
- Ranchu, Oranda, Black Moor, and Bubble Eye remain slow, low-flow, delicate fancy varieties.
- Bubble Eye remains the most restrictive record because its eye sacs and impaired vision create risks not shared by every fancy goldfish.
- Reticulated Hillstream Loach and Siamese Algae Eater retain high-flow traits.
- Weather Loach, White Cloud Mountain Minnow, Zebra Danio, Paradise Fish, and American Flagfish remain meaningfully cooler than warm tropical specialists.

## Deferred

- A generic “fancy goldfish cohort” trait would express compatible slow-bodied varieties more accurately than either universal compatibility or a species-only boolean.
- American Flagfish fin nipping is widely reported in aquarium husbandry but remains context-dependent; no fin-risk flag was added from anecdotal evidence alone.
- Goldfish predation on very small animals is opportunistic and size-dependent. The existing engine should rely on size and feeding traits rather than a named-pair list.

No Supabase synchronization was performed.
