# Species Factual Review — Batch 8: Remaining Specialists

Date: July 2026

This final local-only Phase 11I batch reviewed unusual surface fish, weakly electric fish, delicate schooling fish, and remaining specialist records.

## Corrections

- Wrestling Halfbeak: minimum group increased from 3 to 4. Husbandry references recommend a group of at least 4–6 with careful male-to-female composition.
- Peter's Elephantnose Fish: bottom territory footprint changed from none to medium. FishBase describes it as territorial and usually aggressive toward conspecifics.
- Upside-Down Catfish: minimum group increased from 3 to 5, matching FishBase aquarium guidance.
- Weather Loach: preferred setup changed from stream to planted, resolving the contradiction with its documented still/slow-water habitat.

## Confirmed specialist risks

- Elephantnose and Black Ghost Knifefish remain weakly electric specialists; their sensory and social needs are not reducible to ordinary water-parameter overlap.
- Glass Catfish remain peaceful schooling fish that should not be paired with boisterous or aggressive feeders.
- Wrestling Halfbeaks occupy the surface and have context-dependent male aggression.
- Freshwater Butterflyfish and Golden Wonder Killifish remain surface predators.
- Pea Puffers retain species-only and fin-nipping caution while their precise captive group requirement remains deferred.

## Important schema limitation

Peter's Elephantnose Fish illustrates an “alone or sufficiently large group” requirement: FishBase recommends groups of five or more in a 150 cm aquarium, while undersized groups can suffer severe conspecific aggression. A single `min_group_size` cannot express the safe alternatives. The local record remains solitary-facing rather than forcing casual users into a very large specialist group.

No Supabase synchronization was performed.
